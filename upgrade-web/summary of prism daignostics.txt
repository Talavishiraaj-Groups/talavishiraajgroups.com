# 9. Refund Policy Revision

## Consultation Engagements

Consultation sessions are:

* non-refundable once scheduled
* but may be rescheduled with prior notice

Suggested wording:
“Consultation engagements are non-refundable once scheduled, but may be rescheduled subject to scheduling availability and prior notice.”

---

## Service / Implementation Engagements

Refund structure should now follow a stage-based operational model.

### Before Project Assembly / Work Initiation

Clients are eligible for up to a 90% refund before:

* project assembly
* internal planning
* consultant allocation
* or operational preparation begins

The retained amount covers:

* intermediary costs
* payment processing fees
* administrative allocation
* onboarding overhead

---

### After Project Assembly Begins

Refunds should be calculated according to project stage and completed work scope.

Suggested stage-based structure:

| Project Stage                           | Refund Eligibility  |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Before project assembly                 | Up to 90% refund    |
| Discovery / onboarding initiated        | Up to 75% refund    |
| Research & diagnostic phase active      | Up to 60% refund    |
| Strategy / blueprint development active | Up to 40% refund    |
| Implementation / execution initiated    | Case-by-case review |
| Project substantially completed         | Non-refundable      |

Also add:
“Refund calculations account for consultant allocation, research time, operational planning, intermediary costs, completed work scope, and delivery-stage progression.”

This should sound operationally structured and legally safer.

# 10. Delivery Quality Recovery & Negative Delivery Scorecard

Add a dedicated internal delivery recovery and quality escalation framework.

Purpose:

* improve delivery governance
* operationalize quality control
* create corrective systems for weak engagements
* maintain delivery consistency

The framework should include:

## Trigger Conditions

A delivery review is triggered when:

* client dissatisfaction is expressed
* delivery quality concerns arise
* communication breakdown occurs
* implementation clarity is questioned
* or delivery scores fall below threshold

---

## Review Categories

### 1. Clarity Quality

* Were findings actionable and specific?

### 2. Diagnostic Accuracy

* Was the real operational bottleneck correctly identified?

### 3. Strategic Relevance

* Did recommendations align with company maturity and operational readiness?

### 4. Delivery Professionalism

* responsiveness
* communication
* timeline adherence
* presentation quality
* stakeholder handling

### 5. Actionability

* Could the client realistically execute the recommendations?

## Recovery Actions

Possible corrective actions:

* clarification session
* revised roadmap
* supplemental consultation
* delivery revision
* partial service credit
* internal delivery retraining

This section should strengthen enterprise-grade operational credibility.
