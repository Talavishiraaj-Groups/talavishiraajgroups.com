# 13. Refund Policy — Final Structure

The Refund Policy should exist as a dedicated page.

The tone should feel:

* operationally governed,
* professionally structured,
* legally safer,
* and enterprise-oriented.

It should NOT feel:

* emotional,
* casual,
* or support-ticket styled.

---

## Refund Policy Structure

### A. General Policy Statement

Clarify:

* operational allocation,
* consultant scheduling,
* research commitment,
* operational preparation,
* and engagement planning.

---

### B. Consultation Engagement Refunds

Consultation-based engagements:

* become non-refundable once scheduled,
* but may be rescheduled with sufficient notice and availability.

This protects:

* consultant allocation,
* operational scheduling,
* and engagement preparation.

---

### C. Service & Implementation Refund Structure

Refunds should follow stage-based operational progression.

Recommended structure:

| Project Stage                      | Refund Eligibility |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Before project assembly            | Up to 90%          |
| Discovery/onboarding initiated     | Up to 75%          |
| Research & diagnostic phase active | Up to 60%          |
| Strategy/blueprint phase active    | Up to 40%          |
| Implementation/execution initiated | Case-by-case       |
| Project substantially completed    | Non-refundable     |

---

### D. Refund Calculation Basis

Refund calculations may account for:

* consultant allocation,
* research time,
* operational preparation,
* intermediary costs,
* completed work scope,
* delivery progression,
* administrative overhead,
* and scheduling commitment.

---

### E. Payment Infrastructure

Mention:

* primary global payment infrastructure for international transactions,
* alternative regional methods where applicable,
* international reconciliation timelines,
* and processing dependencies.
