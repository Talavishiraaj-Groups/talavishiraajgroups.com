Cookie notice

Nothing is set in your browser until you answer the banner, and declining keeps it that way. Accepting permits two things and no more: measuring how the site is used, and loading a booking calendar if you go to book a call.

That is a short enough list to set out precisely, so the whole position is below rather than buried in a policy. Everything on this page describes what the site does today, and it was checked in a browser rather than copied from a template.

What this site does and does not do

Cookies set by this site itselfNone.
This domain sets no cookies of its own, for any purpose. The cookies that can appear if you accept belong to the two third parties named below and are set under their own terms.
Data stored in your browser by usYour answer, and nothing else.
One entry holding one word, recording whether you accepted or declined, so you are not asked again. Nothing else is written to local storage or session storage. Clear site data and it goes, along with the record of your answer.
Advertising and cross site trackingNone.
There are no advertising pixels, no remarketing tags and no social network trackers anywhere on the site.
Page view countingCookieless.
Vercel Analytics counts page views without cookies and without building a profile of you. It cannot follow you to other websites and it runs whether or not you accept anything.
Behaviour analyticsOnly if you allow it.
If you accept, Microsoft Clarity shows us which pages hold attention, how far people read, where they go next and where they give up. It sets cookies and can replay a session. Text you type into forms is masked and stays masked. Decline and it is never loaded.
FontsSelf hosted.
Typefaces are served from this domain rather than from a font network, so loading a page does not announce your visit to a third party.
Server logsStandard.
Our hosting provider records ordinary request information such as IP address and user agent, for security and reliability. That is described in the privacy policy.

The third parties, and when they load

Two, and both wait for your answer. Microsoft Clarity measures how the site is used, and loads only if you accept. Cal.com provides the booking calendar inside our page, and loads only if you accept and then go to book. Decline and no request reaches either of them, ever: the scripts are not inserted rather than inserted and idle.

Both set their own cookies under their own terms once loaded. We do not control those and we do not read them. Declining costs you nothing that matters: the contact form reaches us identically and involves nobody else.

What the banner is actually asking

Two things, and they are worth separating. The first is measurement: whether we may see which pages held your attention, how far you read and where you gave up, so the site can be made less annoying than it is. The second is the booking calendar, which runs inside our page and belongs to Cal.com. Decline and neither loads. You can read every page, and the contact form reaches us just as well.

There is an irony in it worth stating rather than hiding. A banner you can dismiss has to remember that you dismissed it, so your answer is the one thing this site does write to your browser. It is a single entry holding a single word, it is readable only by this site, and remembering a consent decision is permitted without asking permission for it. If you clear site data it comes back, because the record of your answer went with it.

Neither answer is made easier than the other, and declining is not a dead end anywhere on the site. The privacy policy sets out what we collect when you contact us, how long it is kept and your rights over it.
