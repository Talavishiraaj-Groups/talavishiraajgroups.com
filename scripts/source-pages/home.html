Why Talavishiraaj Groups

How we are different in practice

01

Research sits in front of engineering

We establish what is true before we decide what to build. It means the work we propose is sometimes not the work we were asked for, and it is why what we build tends to hold.

02

We build what we recommend

Analysis and delivery are the same relationship. That removes the handoff where most initiatives lose momentum, and it stops us recommending anything we could not deliver ourselves.

03

Several disciplines, one team

Engineering, artificial intelligence, product, research and commercial capability work together on the same problem instead of being sequenced through separate suppliers.

04

We are vendor neutral

No reseller relationships shape what we recommend. If the right answer is to keep the systems you already have, that is what we will tell you.

05

The capability ends up yours

Documentation, training and transition planning are part of the work. The measure of a good engagement is that you can run the result without us.
