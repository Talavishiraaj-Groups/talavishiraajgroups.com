About

A multidisciplinary organization built around difficult problems

Talavishiraaj Groups brings research, technology, AI, and engineering together to solve complex problems and build practical systems. We operate across software, automation, product development, and business strategy. Our distributed teams mean we bring the exact expertise your problem needs, regardless of geography.

Why we are built this way

Why we work this way

Problems do not respect professional boundaries

A coordination issue often hides a data problem. A data problem often hides an integration problem. Solving just one part usually just moves the friction somewhere else.

Most of the industry separates these pieces. Advisory work ends at a recommendation and stops before the build starts. Development work delivers exactly what was asked for, even when that is not what solves the problem. Neither side is accountable for whether the thing finally works.

We do both halves, which is the whole point. Our consulting work and our engineering work are the same engagement: we research the problem, design the solution, build the software, and see it through to deployment. The practical consequence is that we only recommend what we know we can build, because we are the ones who will have to build it.

How we think

Three commitments

01

Research comes first

We understand the reality of the problem before deciding what to build. This ensures our solutions actually work in the real world.

02

Evidence drives decisions

We present findings based on evidence, not assumptions. If the data is unclear, we will tell you, rather than making guesses.

03

We build what we recommend

We don’t separate strategy from execution. If we suggest a solution, we take full responsibility for building it.

How work moves

Research through to a working result

    Research

  2. 02

  3. 03

What it is like to work with us

How an engagement actually runs

The arc above is the shape of the work. This is the part people usually want to know before committing: what we need from you, how often you hear from us, and how it ends.

01

How it starts

A conversation, then a written scope. We will not quote before we understand the requirement, because a number produced earlier than that is a number for a different project. Where the decision itself is still open, we will say so and point at the shorter piece of work that settles it rather than selling the larger one.

02

What we need from you

Less time than you expect, and it is specific: access to the systems and the people who know why they work the way they do, and someone empowered to decide when a question needs answering. Engagements slow down for want of a decision far more often than for want of engineering.

03

How often you hear from us

A working session at an agreed cadence, and progress demonstrated rather than described. Where something has slipped it gets raised while there is still room to respond, which is the only point at which that information is useful to you.

04

How it ends

With documentation, runbooks and training that were scoped at the start rather than offered at the end. The measure of the work is that your team can change what was built after we leave. A partner who has become structurally necessary has failed at the job.

How we are organised
