Engineering and AI services: what we build and run

Six core capabilities across research, engineering, AI, product development, and commercial systems. Real problems usually require a mix of these. We keep them under one roof because projects often stall when strategy is handed off to a separate delivery team.

01

Technology & Engineering in detail

What we do

  • CRM, ERP and business system implementation and configuration
  • API design and integration between existing systems
  • Data pipelines, warehousing and reporting infrastructure
  • Architecture review and modernization of systems that have outgrown their design
  • Security, access control and environment hardening

Artificial Intelligence & Automation

We design and implement AI and automation for specific, identified work: research, document handling, workflow routing, decision support and internal operations.

Artificial Intelligence & Automation in detail

What we do

  • Agentic systems: agents that take actions in your tools, with the limits and approvals set first

  • Machine learning models and evaluation pipelines, including forecasting and predictive maintenance
  • Language and document systems: extraction, classification, summarization, generation
  • Computer vision where the problem and the data support it
  • Workflow automation across existing tools and native integrations
  • Decision support with human review at the points that carry risk
  • Moving prototypes into production with observability, fallbacks and staged rollout

What is different here

Six firms will quote you this list. Three things separate how we take it on

01

We will tell you not to build it

The usual problem with asking a build firm whether to build something is that they are paid either way to say yes. We assess volume, repeatability and error cost first, and where the work does not justify itself we say so before a proposal exists.

02

We build what we recommend

The consulting and the engineering are one engagement rather than two suppliers. Where advice ends at a specification, the initiative stalls at exactly that handover. If an operating change needs a reporting layer or an integration built to work, that is part of what you are buying rather than the next supplier’s problem.

03

The engagement is designed to end

Documentation, runbooks and training are scoped at the start, not offered at the end. A partner who becomes structurally necessary has failed at the job. The measure of the work is that your team can change what was built after we leave.

Across sectors

The problem shapes the approach, not the industry

We work across industries where complex problems require deep technical and commercial expertise. Our approach is shaped by the problem, not by a rigid industry template. Whether you operate in manufacturing, software, finance, or research, the fundamental questions remain the same. What changes are your specific constraints: regulation, downtime tolerance, available data, and how you make decisions.
