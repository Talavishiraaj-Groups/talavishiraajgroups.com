# Consulting vocabulary, and verifying the inferred sector phrases

Source: Ahrefs free Keyword Generator, Google, country = United States.
Date: 2026-11-08. Free tier reports volume in bands (>1000, >100, <100) and
KD as Easy / Medium / Hard, so everything below is a band, not a number.
That is enough to rank options against each other, which is all it is used
for here.

---

## 1. The consulting question

The question asked was whether someone searching "consulting" finds us.

### What the site currently says

Every single occurrence of the word on the site describes what we are not:

- "A consultancy hands over a specification and leaves"
- "Consultants provide strategy and leave before the build starts"
- "Not a consultancy deck"
- "How is this different from management consulting?"

Zero titles contain it. Zero H1s contain it. Two meta descriptions contain
it, and in both it refers to somebody else or to refund stages.

So the answer is no, and the position is worse than a simple gap. The entire
footprint of the word is us arguing against the category. An LLM asked to
recommend AI consultants has four sentences of ours that read as us
excluding ourselves.

### What the market actually searches

Seed "ai consulting", US, phrase match: **4,960 keywords**.

| Keyword | KD | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| ai consulting | Hard | >1000 |
| ai consulting services | Medium | >1000 |
| ai consulting company | Hard | >1000 |
| ai consulting firms | Medium | >1000 |
| ai consulting companies | Medium | >1000 |
| ai consulting agency | Hard | >1000 |
| generative ai consulting services | Medium | >1000 |

Seven terms at >1000 in one family. For comparison, the best sector phrase
found anywhere in section 2 below reaches >100. This is an order of
magnitude more demand than every unverified sector put together.

The cost of that demand is difficulty: Hard and Medium here, against Easy
for the sector terms. This is a longer play, not a quick win, and
"ai consulting services" (Medium, >1000, updated within the hour) is the
realistic entry point rather than the bare head term.

| which ai consulting company should i choose | <100 |

Nine of those ten are selection and evaluation questions. Not "what is AI",
not "how do neural networks work": people are standing in front of a choice
and asking how to make it. That is precisely the moment our differentiator
is worth something, because the honest answer to "how do I evaluate AI
consulting firms" includes "find out whether they build what they
recommend", which is the thing we already say.

Two of them map onto pages that already exist:

- "how much do ai consulting services cost?" -> /what-it-costs
- "how to choose an ai consulting partner" -> the existing insight
  "How to Choose a Software Development Partner", which is the right article
  wearing the wrong vocabulary.

### What this does and does not justify

It justifies **stopping the concession**, not claiming the category.

Keep every line about management consulting. McKinsey and BCG strategy work
is genuinely not what we do, the differentiator is true, and chasing it
would be fabrication of the kind already rejected elsewhere in this
research.

Change the framing from "we are not consultants" to "we advise and then
build it". Same claim, same honesty, but it stops reading as a disclaimer
of a category that buyers use as a search word for exactly our work.

## 2. Verifying the inferred sector phrases

Three sectors were verified earlier at Easy and >1000: logistics,
healthcare, fintech. Sixteen were marked `inferred`. Manufacturing was
flagged at the time as the likeliest wrong. That flag was correct.

### Manufacturing: the guess was wrong, and no better phrase rescues it

Seed "manufacturing automation": 843 keywords.

| Keyword | KD | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| manufacturing automation news | Hard | >1000 |
| manufacturing automation technician is it for you | N/A | >1000 |
| manufacturing automation | Easy | >1000 |
| manufacturing automation companies | Easy | >100 |
| manufacturing automation solutions | Hard | >100 |
| manufacturing automation trends 2026 | N/A | >100 |

The two highest-volume terms are a trade publication and a careers
question. Our actual target phrase, "manufacturing automation companies",
sits at >100, a band below the verified three.

Seed "manufacturing software development": only **23 keywords**, every one
at >100. No variant reaches >1000.

This is the seventh appearance of the career-intent pattern recorded in this
research. "manufacturing automation technician is it for you" is someone
choosing a trade, not someone buying engineering.

**Verdict: no phrase found reaches the bar. Manufacturing's targetPhrase is
not supportable at >1000 and should stay marked as unverified rather than be
quietly promoted.**

### IoT: real but small

Seed "iot development company": 56 keywords.

| Keyword | KD | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| iot development company | Easy | >100 |
| custom iot development company | Easy | >100 |
| iot development company usa | Easy | <100 |
| blockchain iot development company | N/A | <100 |

Questions tab: **3 keywords**, all <100.

Easy to rank, and the intent is clean buyer intent with no career or news
contamination. But the ceiling is >100, and three questions is close to no
question demand at all.

**Verdict: genuine but minor. Worth keeping as a page, not worth treating as
a growth driver.**

## 3. The pattern worth recording

The three verified sectors reach >1000. Every unverified sector phrase
checked so far tops out at >100, an order of magnitude lower, and the free
tool's bands cannot distinguish between them below that.

The honest reading is that "X Software Development Company" is a template,
and a template produces phrases in proportion to how large and how
software-literate the sector already is. Logistics, healthcare and fintech
are large and software-literate. The rest are not, or not yet, or they use
different words for it that the template cannot guess.

That is an argument for the consulting vocabulary in section 1 over any
further sector expansion: one family there carries more demand than the
sixteen remaining sector pages can between them.
