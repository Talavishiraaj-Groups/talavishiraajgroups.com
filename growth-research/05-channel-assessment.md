# 05 — Channel assessment

Every channel is judged against the actual constraints, not against best practice in
the abstract. The constraints are:

- **6 people**, one year old, founder is also the delivery capacity. [FACT]
- **143 LinkedIn followers.** No audience anywhere. [FACT]
- **No non-branded organic surface area** — 26 pages, all brand/transactional. [FACT]
- **Zero published proof** — no named client, no figure, no testimonial. [FACT]
- **No verified domain authority, index status or traffic baseline.** Unknown.
- Founder's delivery time is the scarcest resource, and every channel below costs it.

A channel is only "applicable" if it can produce a qualified conversation **before**
the company has an audience. That test eliminates most of the usual list.

---

## Tier 1 — Applicable now, and the evidence says so

### 1. Big-5 content on the operations-diagnostic beachhead
**Evidence:** the SERP for fixed-price ops audits is held entirely by boutiques and
solo operators; Covision ranks twice with one bylined, tool-bearing, price-disclosing
article (`03` Part B). [FACT] No authority moat observed. [OBS]

**Why it fits:** it is the only channel where the *content is also the proof*. A firm
with no case studies can still demonstrate judgement in public. Sheridan's Big 5
supplies the backlog; the company's governance depth and diagnostic method supply the
substance.

**The hard part, stated honestly:** this needs the founder's own thinking, at length,
under his own name. It cannot be outsourced or generated, because the only asset being
traded is demonstrated judgement. [INFER] That is a real cost to delivery capacity and
it is the main reason this plan could fail.

### 2. An ungated diagnostic instrument
**Evidence:** `ai readiness assessment` completes to `tool`, `questionnaire`,
`template`, `framework`, `methodology` — searchers want an instrument. [FACT] Covision
embeds three, labelled "no email required," and ranks twice. [FACT]

**Why it fits:** this is Prism's methodology as a free, self-serve artefact. It
converts a diagnostic *service* — unsearchable — into a diagnostic *tool* that is
searchable, linkable, shareable and quotable. It is the one asset that serves SEO,
link-earning, LinkedIn, Reddit, partnerships and conversion simultaneously.
It is also the single strongest Perceived-Likelihood device available to a firm with
no case studies: it lets the buyer experience the method before paying. [INFER]

Ungated, on the evidence. The email is worth less than the trust.

### 3. Founder-led LinkedIn
**Evidence:** the founder already writes well and publishes a genuine point of view
("research before execution; diagnose before prescription; structure before scale");
his reflective posts are his best existing asset. [FACT] The market explicitly rewards
named humans — every ranking competitor page is bylined. [OBS]

**Why it fits:** zero cost, no gatekeeper, and it is where founder-led B2B buyers
already are. Also the only channel that compounds while the site has no traffic.

**Correction required:** distribution currently runs through the *company* page (143
followers, corporate-voice posts about "R&D officially beginning"). [FACT] The
founder's personal profile is the asset; the company page is not. [INFER]

### 4. Service-directory listings
**Evidence:** the SERP for `ai automation agency for small businesses` *is* directories
— DesignRush and G2 rank on page one. [FACT]

**Why it fits:** highest effort-to-visibility ratio available. One-off setup, no
content, no audience needed, and it places the company inside SERPs it cannot rank in
directly. Also supplies the citation/link baseline the domain currently lacks.

**Caveat:** several of these monetise placement. Budget and expected quality unknown —
verify before paying. Listing ≠ leads. [HYP]

### 5. Partnerships with fractional COOs and boutique ops consultants
**Evidence:** the market is full of solo fractional COOs (ELSE/Alex Boch verified;
Umbrex is a whole network of them). [FACT]

**Why it fits, and why it is the most under-rated item here:** they are not rivals.
They sell a *person on retainer*; Prism sells a *fixed-scope artefact*. They need a
defensible diagnosis to justify and scope a retainer, and producing one themselves is
unbillable overhead. Prism is that diagnosis, white-labellable, in 10 days.

This is the only Tier 1 channel that **requires no audience, no traffic and no content
at all** — it needs perhaps 30 accurate conversations. For a company with 143 followers
that is a decisive advantage, and it is likely the fastest route to first revenue.
[INFER — the economic logic is sound on both sides; the conversion rate is untested]

### 6. Reddit and practitioner communities
**Evidence:** `ai automation agency reddit`, `n8n agency reddit`, `ai automation for
small businesses reddit` all complete — buyers actively route around vendor sites.
[FACT] n8n's community alone is ~200,000 members and free to join. [FACT]

**Why it fits:** it is where the trust decision is actually made, and Sheridan's
"disarmament" register is exactly what those threads reward.

**Non-negotiable:** participate as the founder, answer specific questions, no links,
no pitching. Anything else gets removed and damages the name. This channel punishes
marketing more reliably than any other.

## Tier 2 — Conditionally applicable; sequence behind Tier 1

### 7. Geo-targeted service pages (Bengaluru, UK)
Geography is the strongest intent modifier observed (`02` Finding 4a). [FACT] The
company has genuine standing in both. [FACT]

**Condition:** do this only with real substance per location — jurisdiction, address,
a named person, local work. Thin city pages are the commonest low-quality SEO pattern
and would undercut the trust positioning. Also unresolved: whether the incumbents
winning `...company in bangalore` are doing so with quality or with scaled spam
(`03` Open). Resolve that first. [HYP]

### 8. First-party research publication
Covision's hook is a proprietary statistic from its own 30+ audits. [FACT] The same
device is available to Talavishiraaj — anonymised patterns across its own diagnostics
are publishable, defensible, quotable and link-earning.

**Condition:** requires enough completed engagements for the finding to be honest.
With six case studies, n is too small to publish a statistic. [INFER] Do not
manufacture one. Revisit at ~20 engagements. Until then publish *method and pattern*
without numbers.

### 9. Guest writing and podcasts
Realistic and cheap for a founder with a genuine thesis, and it earns the editorial
links the domain lacks. But it converts on the strength of the thesis — so it is
gated behind Tier 1 sharpening the thesis first. Sequence, don't skip.

## The channel logic, in one line

The company has no audience, so every channel that *needs* an audience is unavailable.
What remains is: **borrow other people's distribution** (directories, partners,
communities, guest platforms) while **building one owned asset that is also proof** (a
diagnostic instrument surrounded by Big-5 content, published under a named human).

> [INFER] Tier 1 #5 (fractional-COO partnerships) is the fastest path to revenue and
> needs nothing built. Tier 1 #1 + #2 is the slowest but the only one that compounds.
> Running #5 to fund the patience required for #1/#2 is the sequencing this evidence
> supports.
