# Talavishiraaj Groups — Growth & Inbound Acquisition Research

Independent research project. Goal: establish how Talavishiraaj Groups becomes
discoverable, trusted and commercially relevant to people who do not know it,
and how that discovery converts into qualified inbound enquiries and revenue.

**Rule of this folder:** every claim is tagged.
- `[FACT]` — directly observed (fetched page, API response, live SERP, filing).
- `[OBS]` — observed pattern across several sources, not a single hard datum.
- `[INFER]` — reasoned conclusion from facts. Could be wrong.
- `[HYP]` — hypothesis requiring a test. Not yet evidence.
- `[UNVERIFIED]` — claim found in a source I could not confirm. Do not act on.

No traffic numbers, search volumes, rankings, conversion rates or competitor
results are invented. Where a number is unknown, it says unknown.
